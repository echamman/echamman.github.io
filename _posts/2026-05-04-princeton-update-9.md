This week I worked on finishing the real woodworking portion of the build. 

I started by putting everything together to get an idea of what the finished product will look like. Looking promising!
![semi_assembled_2](/assets/img/update9/semi_assembled_2.jpg)

Next up I drilled the holes for the speaker and inserted the tee nuts. 
![tee_nuts](/assets/img/update9/speaker_baffle_tees.jpg)
![speaker_mounted](/assets/img/update9/speaker_mounted.jpg)

I then added the inner supports. These are for screwing the back panels and speaker baffle on later. Pretty straightforward, I just used wood glue to attach these. 
![inner_supports1](/assets/img/update9/clamping_inner_wood.jpg)
![inner_supports2](/assets/img/update9/clamping_inner_wood_2.jpg)

Now for a fun part! I used the router with a roundover bit to round the edges. Did a test run first to get the depth right. 
![round_test](/assets/img/update9/rounded_edge_test.jpg)

Then I went at the cabinet. I rounded every outer edge except for the top front because it's an obtuse angle and the router only works on 90 degree angles. I think I'll take sandpaper to this. 
![rounded1](/assets/img/update9/rounded_edge_1.jpg)
![rounded2](/assets/img/update9/rounded_edge_2.jpg)

Lastly I drilled small holes on the baffle and back panels for the wood screws to go in later. 

![mounting_holes1](/assets/img/update9/baffle_holes.jpg)
![mounting_holes2](/assets/img/update9/rear_panel_holes.jpg)

Lastly I put everything together! The speaker baffle is only taped on for now, and it's a little wonky. I plan to adjust the chassis mount to line everything up a little better. Thankfully the tolex will cover the extra holes

![assembled](/assets/img/update9/semi_assembled.jpg)

It sounds even better in a wood frame compared to the cardboard, it absolutely rocks! I played it all weekend, going to be hard to take it apart to finish the job. 

Next update should be tolex and cloth!
